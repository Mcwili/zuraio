Integrations

Works alongside the systems your team already uses.

Zuraio is designed to connect company knowledge and workflows without forcing teams to replace the systems that already work.

Methods

A flexible integration layer.

Zuraio can connect approved systems and capabilities through MCP, APIs, webhooks and customer-specific connectors. The available integrations depend on the selected use case, deployment and current product status.

MCP

Standardised access to approved assistant and tool capabilities.

APIs

Connections to business applications, internal services and data sources through defined interfaces.

Webhooks and events

Support for selected asynchronous workflows and system events.

Customer-specific connectors

Project-based connections for specialist systems and internal applications.

Categories

Integration categories.

The categories below describe the types of systems Zuraio is designed to connect. Availability must be confirmed for each customer setup.

E-mail and calendar

Outlook, Gmail and calendar environments, subject to current integration status.

Productivity and documents

Microsoft 365, SharePoint, OneDrive, Google Workspace and approved document repositories, subject to current integration status.

Collaboration and messaging

Microsoft Teams, Slack and other approved communication platforms, subject to current integration status.

CRM, ERP and specialist systems

Customer, finance, project and operational systems — including Abacus, bexio, SAP, Dynamics 365, Odoo, Sage, Proffix and Klara — through supported or customer-specific interfaces.

APIs and internal applications

REST APIs, webhooks, databases, internal tools and customer-specific services.

Contact us to confirm the current status and available scope of a particular integration.
